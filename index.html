01 / Scholarship

Research

From measuring cardiac function to guiding a biopsy, my research looks at what medical imaging can reveal and how it can support clinical decisions.

Cardiovascular MRI & PET/MR

I studied how to quantify myocardial perfusion with simultaneous PET/MR and how to use MRI to evaluate cell therapy after myocardial infarction. I also developed methods to recover ECG signals during MRI-guided electrophysiology.

Optical imaging

In collaboration with MD Anderson, I helped develop real-time optical coherence tomography (OCT) image processing for tumor segmentation and presented related work on core needle biopsy guidance at SPIE Photonics West.

Image-guided robotics

At Canon’s Healthcare Optics Research Laboratory, I led the integration of CT-based path planning, registration, and robotic navigation in 3D Slicer for bronchoscopy.
